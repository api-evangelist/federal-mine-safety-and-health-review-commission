# Federal Mine Safety and Health Review Commission

> The Federal Mine Safety and Health Review Commission (FMSHRC) is an independent U.S.
> adjudicative agency that provides administrative trial and appellate review of legal
> disputes arising under the Federal Mine Safety and Health Amendments Act of 1977 (the
> Mine Act). It adjudicates contests of citations, orders and civil penalties issued by
> the Department of Labor's Mine Safety and Health Administration (MSHA); it does not
> regulate mining or enforce the Mine Act itself.

## Machine-readable surface

FMSHRC publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-09-09 there is no developer program, no
OpenAPI/Swagger/GraphQL/AsyncAPI contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK on any
public package registry, and no /.well-known/ discovery document on any FMSHRC host. The
public website is Drupal 10 with its JSON:API and REST modules disabled: /jsonapi, /api,
/rest, /graphql, /data.json and /openapi.json all return 404, and a node fetched with
?_format=json returns HTTP 406 ("A route that returns a rendered array as its response only
supports the HTML format"). Case documents and decisions are published as HTML pages and
PDFs on fmshrc.gov, not through a programmable interface.

The agency's Electronic Case Management System (e-CMS) is a vendor-hosted tenant at
fmshrc-ecms.entellitrak.com (Entellitrak, Tyler Technologies). Its root redirects to a
credentialed login form. It is a filing application for parties to a case, not an API, and
anything it served would belong to the vendor rather than to FMSHRC.

The one machine-consumable feed the site does publish is a site-content RSS feed at
https://www.fmshrc.gov/rss.xml (application/rss+xml).
